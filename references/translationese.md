# Persian Translationese Reference

Use this reference when Persian is grammatical but reads like an English sentence mapped into Persian. Translationese is structural and constructional; it is not a blacklist of borrowed words.

## Diagnostic principle

Do not diagnose translationese from sentence length, lexical diversity, connective count, or one phrase. Diagnose a local construction only when Persian reference, information flow, predicate choice, or discourse organization is weaker because source-language structure appears to have been preserved.

## 1. Overt-subject carryover

Example:

`او تصمیم گرفت که او پروژه را ادامه دهد.`

If the subject remains stable and the verb/context already identify it, the second `او` may be unnecessary.

Repair:

`او تصمیم گرفت پروژه را ادامه دهد.`

Keep the explicit pronoun when it marks contrast, emphasis, switch-reference, or resolves ambiguity.

## 2. Repeated lexical subject

Example:

`شرکت اعلام کرد... شرکت اضافه کرد... شرکت توضیح داد...`

When the same actor remains topical, Persian may omit or reduce later mentions.

Do not remove repeated names in news, legal, or technical text when attribution clarity matters.

## 3. Literal predicate calque

Example:

`این ویژگی نقش مهمی بازی می‌کند.`

Possible repair:

`این ویژگی نقش مهمی دارد.`

The problem is the construction, not the words `نقش` or `مهم`. Rewrite the predicate as a unit.

## 4. Literal "allow" frame

Example:

`این قابلیت به کاربران اجازه می‌دهد که آن‌ها فایل را آپلود کنند.`

Possible repair:

`این قابلیت به کاربران اجازه می‌دهد فایل را آپلود کنند.`

Keep the embedded subject when it differs from the matrix object or is needed for clarity.

## 5. Demonstrative-shell scaffolding

Example:

`این موضوع باعث ... می‌شود. این موضوع همچنین ...`

If `این موضوع` merely carries the previous proposition forward, remove it or use a clearer referential form.

Do not remove it when categorizing the antecedent as an actual issue/problem/matter contributes meaning.

## 6. Heavy noun chain

Example:

`فرایند انجام ارزیابی عملکرد سامانه`

Possible repairs depend on meaning:

- `ارزیابی عملکرد سامانه`
- `عملکرد سامانه را ارزیابی می‌کنیم`

Never invent a first-person actor that the source does not license.

## 7. Light-verb and administrative inflation

Example:

`نسبت به انجام بررسی اقدام نمود.`

Possible repair:

`بررسی کرد.`

Keep fixed institutional, legal, or procedural language where the form itself has genre value.

## 8. Participial scaffolding

Example:

`با ارائه X، موجب ایجاد Y می‌شود.`

Repair only after confirming the source actually claims that X causes Y. A smoother causal sentence is still wrong if the original relation was merely associative.

## 9. Passive organization copied from English

Example:

`این تغییر توسط تیم محصول اعمال شد.`

If the team is the salient known actor:

`تیم محصول این تغییر را اعمال کرد.`

Keep the passive when the changed object is the discourse topic or the actor is irrelevant.

## 10. Over-explicit logical relation

A translated passage may verbalize every addition, contrast, cause, and consequence.

Review combinations such as:

`علاوه بر این ... بنابراین ... در نتیجه ...`

Remove only the marker whose function is already obvious. Formal argument may legitimately need explicit inference markers.

## 11. English rhetorical sequence

Common translated macrostructure:

generic background → importance statement → roadmap → content → generic conclusion

In a short Persian article, lead with the real proposition if the framing adds no needed context.

Keep explicit scope and roadmap in long reports, research introductions, and navigation-heavy documents when they serve a real function.

## 12. Punctuation transfer

English comma logic, colon use, quotation style, or em-dash chains can survive translation even after syntax changes.

Re-punctuate only after the Persian clause structure is settled. Respect house style and protected quotations.

## 13. Terminology over-translation

Technical Persian can sound less natural when established English terms are replaced with unfamiliar equivalents.

Protect community-standard terms, product names, API names, commands, package names, flags, and identifiers. Edit the Persian syntax around them instead.

## Verification questions

Before accepting a translationese repair:

- Is the referent still unambiguous?
- Did contrast or emphasis change?
- Did the actor change?
- Did a correlation become a cause?
- Did a technical term change?
- Did the sentence become more casual or more formal than the genre?
- Was the original construction actually unnatural, or merely formal?

If the answer is uncertain, prefer `FLAG` or `KEEP` over a confident rewrite.
